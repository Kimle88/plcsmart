FAQ

Questions engineers ask

Can I download the generated program straight into a running machine?

No. PLCSmart produces a draft to speed up your work. Like any code from a colleague, it must be reviewed, simulated and tested by a qualified engineer — including all safety functions — before it goes anywhere near real equipment.

Which software can import the exports?

Siemens projects export as SCL or AWL/STL source for TIA Portal, Mitsubishi as source for GX Works, and Allen Bradley as an L5X routine for Studio 5000. Each export opens an import guide with the exact steps.

What happens to my descriptions and uploaded manuals?

Your requirement text is sent to our AI provider only to generate the program. For uploaded PDF manuals, text is extracted in your browser and an excerpt is sent to the AI provider to build your knowledge base; the knowledge base itself is stored in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 generations per month with no credit card required.
