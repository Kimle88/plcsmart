Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what data PLCSmart ("we"), operated by [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, name and a hashed password.
  • Usage data: number of generations per month and programs you choose to save to your history.
  • Content you submit: program descriptions, I/O tables and chat messages.
  • Knowledge Base: PDF manuals are read in your browser; a text excerpt is sent for analysis and the resulting knowledge base is stored in your browser, not on our servers.

How we use it

To provide the service, enforce plan limits, provide support and improve reliability. We do not sell personal data.

Content you submit for generation or chat is sent to our AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to produce the response.

Retention & deletion
